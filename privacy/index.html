Privacy

Short version: I keep your email to send you the free piece and an occasional note, and nothing else. Updated October 31, 2026.

What I collect

  • Your email address, only if you type it into the sign-up form.
  • Basic visit counts through GoatCounter: which page was viewed and which site sent you. No cookies, no ad trackers, and nothing that identifies you personally.

Why

To email you the weekly free piece, a making tip, and now and then a discount code for the Pixel x Pigment shop on Etsy. You sign up yourself and confirm by email; nobody is added to the list any other way, and Etsy customers are never added from their orders.

Who handles it

The email list is kept with Kit (kit.com), which sends the emails. Your address is never sold, rented or shared with anyone else. The site's fonts and images are served from this site itself.

Leaving and your rights

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom; one click removes you. You can also ask to see, correct or delete your data by messaging the Pixel x Pigment shop on Etsy.

Juniper's Corner is the little free-art room of Pixel x Pigment, a watercolor clipart shop on Etsy.
