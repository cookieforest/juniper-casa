Who's Juniper?

A duck with a paintbrush

Juniper is the name and illustrated face of Pixel x Pigment: a small white duck in a green beret who never puts the paintbrush down.

Pixel x Pigment makes watercolor clipart for people who make things: cards, stickers, invitations, planners and small-shop products. Each collection sticks to one palette, so every piece works with the others.

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Leave your email, tap the confirm button in the first message, and the download link follows. No payment and no account.

What can I do with the free piece?

Use it in your own personal projects: cards, gifts, planners, crafts and home decor. If you'd like to sell what you make, the full collection on Etsy comes with a small-business commercial license.

How are the pieces made?

Each collection is designed with digital and AI-assisted tools, then every piece is hand-selected, cleaned up and finished, and checked at full size and at thumbnail size before it goes out.

Will you fill up my inbox?

No. At most one email a week, each with a one-click unsubscribe. Your email is never sold or shared. Privacy details.

Something went wrong. How do I get help?

Send a message through the Pixel x Pigment shop on Etsy and I'll sort it out quickly.
